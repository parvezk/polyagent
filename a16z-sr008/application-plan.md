## Interview prompts
1. Tell me about the last task where you used more than one coding agent.
2. What caused you to switch or use agents in parallel?
3. What did you manually copy, check, repeat, or recover? What was the consequence?
4. How often does that happen? What do you use today to manage it?
5. Could you show the workflow? Would you try PolyAgent on your next real task?

Do not lead with a feature tour. Interest is not usage; usage is not retention; willingness to try is not willingness to pay. If conversations cannot happen before submission, state what is known and the next test honestly.
