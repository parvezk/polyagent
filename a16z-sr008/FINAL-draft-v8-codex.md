# PolyAgent: application draft v8

## One-liner (100/100)

PolyAgent helps developers track, steer and review coding agents across platforms in one place.

## Startup description (769/800)

PolyAgent is a multi-agent orchestration workspace for developers working across AI platforms such as Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Antigravity. It brings coding-agent runs into one panel with a human-in-the-loop queue.

I built it from my own sprawl across design and development. I pick the best agent for each job, and by the end of a week I had sessions from four vendors stalled, waiting on me, each in a different dashboard. Returning the next day meant reconstructing where I had stopped.

Today, PolyAgent dispatches tasks, tracks runs and sends follow-ups across vendors. The queue separates work needing my input from work ready for review. Next, it will explain changes and decisions needed, so a developer can direct a team of agents without carrying the whole project in their head.

## Relevant experience (943/1000)

At Amazon, as a Staff Front-End Engineer, I owned the team architecture for user-facing products across Middle Mile technology. I proposed Synapse and led its front-end architecture, building an agent-driven interface that answers facility questions with dynamic, interactive visualizations.

I collaborated with operations teams to launch Right Station, from planning and A/B testing through rollout across 253 fulfillment centers and 110 sort centers.

In 2019, I co-founded DeepViz from my NYU master's research, exploring methods to interpret and understand deep neural networks. We were accepted into NYC Media Lab's six-week Combine accelerator with a $10,000 grant. DeepViz used interactive visualization so a model's reasoning could be inspected.

PolyAgent brings together my passion for multi-agent systems, UI design for AI harnesses, agentic infrastructure and durable workflows. I document my approach at designlogic.substack.com.

## Tell us more about the team (965/1000)

I am the solo founder, working full-time. I designed and built PolyAgent's current product myself and demoed it at Vercel Ship NYC in June 2026.

My experience connects interface design with the agent infrastructure underneath it. Explainable AI and visual interpretability run through my work. I care about the moment someone looks at an AI system's work and decides whether to trust it. PolyAgent brings that question to developers directing teams of agents, where understanding the work informs what happens next.

I'm looking for a co-founder with agent infrastructure experience who has taken a developer product to market. Finding that person is one reason I'm applying to Speedrun. I have no advisors today.

## Anything else (957/1000)

I want PolyAgent to become the workspace where a developer directs an entire project across agents, from design exploration through release. Builders should be able to choose the right agent for each task and budget while keeping a shared view of the work. My ambition is to connect those separate agent environments into a workflow the developer can oversee and steer.

Generative interfaces and dynamic visualization are central to how I want to build it. My aim is to make complex agent workflows easier to reason about, so developers can judge whether the work matches their intent, catch a wrong turn and decide what is ready to ship. I'm starting with coding agents and will put the review workflow in developers' hands to test whether it reduces the coordination they do themselves.
