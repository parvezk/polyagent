# PolyAgent founder video: working script

## 1. Introduce yourself and the product | About 40–45 seconds | Camera

Hi, I'm Parvez, founder of PolyAgent. I'm building a workspace for developers to orchestrate multiple coding agents running in parallel across AI platforms.

PolyAgent gives developers a shared view of agent work, so they can follow progress without context switching between separate AI platforms and step in where needed. In my own workflow, I use Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Antigravity, each with its own agent environment. PolyAgent combines all that work into one interface while letting me keep using the agents I choose.

For example, its review queue surfaces sessions that need my input, so I can respond without checking every platform's agent panel or agent manager dashboard.

## 2. Explain what brought you here | About 15–20 seconds | Camera

In my own workflow, design and dev/coding tasks were spread across different AI systems. I was moving artifacts and instructions between them, carry over context and markdown files, reading through separate sessions to figure out what's the next step. That coordination kept pulling me away from building (and focussing on what matters).

## 3. Show one concrete workflow | About 15–20 seconds | Screen share

This is PolyAgent's queue. It brings runs from connected platforms into one view and separates work waiting on me from work ready to review. I can reply to a question here, or open the work to inspect it.

Screen actions: start on the real queue, point to Waiting on you, open a relevant item and demonstrate Reply only if tested; then point to Ready to review and Open. Show one sequence slowly enough to follow. If there is no safe real reply to send, open the reply field without sending and say “I can reply here.” Do not stage fake live results. Prepared sample data must be labelled as a demo. Avoid private code, credentials or confidential messages.

## 4. Establish founder fit and ambition | About 25–30 seconds | Camera

At Amazon, I built generative interfaces and interactive visualizations for warehouse operations. Before that, I co-founded DeepViz from my NYU research on explainable AI. I've kept coming back to AI Interpretability and excited about understanding what's happening inside AI systems.

I'm building PolyAgent full-time. My ambition is to let developers direct agentic work across harnesses, with the visibility to question their output and have the right control to steer it.

## Recording notes

- Treat this as spoken wording to try, not a paragraph to recite perfectly. Keep contractions and the phrases that sound natural to you.
- The spoken script is approximately 250 words. The longer opening replaces the earlier 18-second version; allow roughly 90–110 seconds with pauses. Review section 2 for overlap before recording.
- Platform examples such as Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Antigravity can appear in the problem discussion if needed. Do not imply every named platform is already integrated. For the demo, name only connections visibly working.
- “Ready to review” and opening outputs are current queue concepts. Do not say PolyAgent automatically verifies changes, determines safety, approves/merges work or explains every change unless demonstrated and verified.
- Keep the camera opening and closing even if the middle uses screen share. The goal is to hear the founder's thinking and see the actual product.
- If a screen demonstration is not ready, keep the same camera script and replace section 3 with a clearly labelled static queue screenshot, describing visible current UI rather than pretending to interact live.
- No new market claims, customer traction or comparative model rankings are added.
