PolyAgent helps developers track, understand and approve coding agents across vendors in one place

## Startup description (736/800)

PolyAgent is a cross-vendor orchestration workspace for developers running multiple coding agents. It brings their work into one panel so developers can see what needs attention and keep projects moving.

I built it after running agents across design and development. The next morning, I had to reconstruct where I had stopped, reread long responses and find which sessions needed me. Even my scheduled agents piled up waiting for clarification.

The current product dispatches tasks, tracks runs and sends follow-ups across vendors. I am building toward an interface that explains what changed and presents the decision needed to continue. I want developers to direct a team of agents without carrying the whole project in their heads.

## Relevant experience (930/1000)

At Amazon, I proposed Synapse after seeing warehouse operators piece together decisions across disconnected dashboards. I led its front-end architecture and guided three engineers, building a conversational interface that generates interactive charts and tables around an operator's question. After a 200-user trial, V1 began rolling out across a network of 253 fulfillment centers and 110 sort centers.

In 2019, I co-founded DeepViz from my NYU master's research. We completed NYC Media Lab's six-week Combine accelerator, pitched at demo day and won a $10,000 grant. DeepViz used visualization to help people inspect how neural networks reached their decisions.

In my own workflow, I orchestrate specialist agents from research through implementation and review. I've customized Claude Code Agent Teams and built hooks and reusable skills to reduce manual coordination. I publish these experiments at designlogic.substack.com.

## Tell us more about the team (871/1000)

I am the solo founder, working full-time. I designed and built PolyAgent's current product myself and demoed it at Vercel Ship NYC in June 2026.

My experience connects interface design with the engineering underneath it. At Amazon, I led engineers and worked with product, design and operations to take Synapse from a proposal into a trial with real users. With DeepViz, I went through the early startup process with co-founders. I bring both experiences to PolyAgent.

I care about the moment someone looks at an AI system's work and has to decide whether to trust it. That question has followed me from my research into the products I build. PolyAgent gives me the chance to work on it for developers directing teams of agents.

I have no co-founder or advisors today. I'm open to a co-founder who has grown a developer product and wants to build this company with me.

## Anything else (985/1000)

I want PolyAgent to become the workspace where a developer can direct an entire project across agents, from the first design exploration through a release. The work should remain understandable as it moves between tools, and returning to it tomorrow should not mean reconstructing yesterday.

Generative interfaces and dynamic visualization are central to that vision. I want the interface to show the artifact under review alongside the context needed to make a decision. My work on Synapse convinced me to keep pursuing interfaces that adapt to the work in front of the user.

I'm starting with coding agents. The next step is to put the review workflow in developers' hands and learn whether it helps them get back to useful work faster. PolyAgent has no external users or revenue yet, and I have not incorporated.
